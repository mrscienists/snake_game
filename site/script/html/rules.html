HOW TO PLAY

• You must collect aples to grow up.
• You can't stop, and you must avoid walls and yourself, if you do not want to lose.
• You can win by collecting all apples and filling every single cell of the grid with your body.
• On the grid can spawn differents apples: red, blue and pink:
red - default apple, gives you +1 point
blue - rare apple, gives you +5 points
pink - very rare apple, gives you +10 points
• Points are used for player rankings on the leaderboards page.

CONTROLS

You are controlling a snake using WASD
W - turn to the top
S - turn down
A - turn left
R - turn right
